Alice behind a NAT connects to sun using IKEv2 with MOBIKE and IP-TFS mode.
The connection is established with ESP-in-UDP encapsulation. A large ping of
1472 bytes, maximum size ping to fit into 1500 bytes mtu, is sent, causing
IPTFS to split the inner IP packet across multiple ESP frames at the origin
<b>alice</b>. The NAT mapping on moon is then changed to trigger a MOBIKE

Endpoint update. After migration, IP-TFS connectivity is verified again with
another large ping.
