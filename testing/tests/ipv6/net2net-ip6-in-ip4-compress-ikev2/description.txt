An IKEv2 tunnel between gateways <b>moon</b> and <b>sun</b> connects two IPv6 subnets over
IPv4, using ESP for encryption and IPComp for compression. Without IPComp, the ESP SA is
in tunnel mode and adds the IPv4 outer header. With IPComp, the bundle is switched:
IPComp takes tunnel mode and adds the outer header, while ESP is installed in transport
mode — only one SA in a bundle can add the outer header. Both SAs carry IPv4 endpoints
but match IPv6 inner traffic, requiring XFRM_STATE_AF_UNSPEC on both. When <b>alice</b>
pings <b>bob</b>, packets travel as IPv4 | ESP | IPComp | IPv6. Authentication uses
X.509 certificates. Two ping sizes are used: a small packet to establish the tunnel and
a larger one to verify compression, staying within the reduced MTU of the IPv4 outer.
